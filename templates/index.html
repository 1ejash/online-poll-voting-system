ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Making Online Polling Simple

The Online Poll and Voting System is a web-based application designed to make polling and voting easier, faster and more organized.

Registered users can participate in active polls, while administrators can manage polls and monitor voting activity.

The system also provides graphical result analysis, allowing users to understand voting outcomes quickly.
